This tracks whether email sent from your domains is passing authentication checks, and tells you what to do next for each one.

❓ What do these terms mean?
  • p=none -- just watching, nothing is blocked or sent to spam yet.
  • p=quarantine -- suspicious mail (failing checks) is sent to spam.
  • p=reject -- suspicious mail is blocked outright, doesn't arrive at all.
  • pct= -- what percentage of your mail this rule applies to. You normally start low (e.g. 10%) and raise it gradually once you've confirmed your real mail keeps passing.
  • Pass rate -- the percentage of your mail that's passing authentication (DKIM/SPF). Higher is better; this is what "safe to move forward" is based on.
  • Live DNS vs. Reports -- "Live DNS" is what's actually published right now; "Reports" is what mail providers have told us they've seen. These can disagree for a few days after you make a change -- that's normal, not necessarily a problem.
  • Overall vibe (Good/Bad/Ugly) -- one combined read per domain: your pass rate, Google's own reported spam rate, and your bounce/complaint rate, weighted into a single score out of 100 (80+ is Good, 50-79 is Bad, below 50 is Ugly). Meant to answer "how's this domain doing" at a glance without reading four separate numbers.
