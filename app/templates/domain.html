✍️ Gmail sender requirements (SPF & DKIM)

Checks against Google's published sender guidelines that don't need any account or API key -- just DNS. SPF allows at most 10 DNS lookups per check (RFC 7208); going over silently breaks SPF with no warning -- mail that should pass starts failing on the sending side, even though DNS still "looks fine" at a glance. DKIM keys must be at least 1024 bits (Google recommends 2048); a weak or missing key means Gmail can't confirm your mail wasn't tampered with in transit. Only checked for domains/selectors your own reports show real, ongoing volume from -- not one-off historical stragglers.
