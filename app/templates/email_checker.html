📧 Email checker

Self-hosted, no subscription, no third-party API -- checks syntax, MX records, known disposable/throwaway providers, and then a real SMTP conversation with the mail server itself (connect, say hello, ask "would you accept mail for this address?", then disconnect -- no message is ever actually sent). Same pipeline every commercial verifier runs. It rules out "this address can't possibly work" and confirms "the mail server says yes right now" -- it can't promise zero future bounces (a full mailbox is a temporary state, some servers accept everything and only bounce later, and a result can genuinely change week to week), but it catches the large majority of dead addresses before you ever send to them.
